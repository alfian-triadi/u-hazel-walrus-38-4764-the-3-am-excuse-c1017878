One line, in the voice of a hardware wallet that will not sign a transaction at 3 AM:

> "No. At 3 AM my job is protecting your coins from you — come back at sunrise, when your brain clocks back in."

108 characters. The wallet's enemy isn't a dead battery or a firmware update — it's its owner's 3 AM brain.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line (no trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), validates it is 1–200 characters, and verifies the written bytes match.
